# Weare User Research Pack v1.0 — Legal Review Checklist

**Status:** Human legal review required before field use
**Purpose:** Organise counsel review of the current research materials. This checklist is not legal advice, legal approval, or a substitute for review by qualified counsel.

## Review instructions

- Review the current wording in the cited file and section together with any linked workflow.
- Do not mark an item approved unless the reviewer has authority to approve it.
- Use one approval state per item: `Pending`, `Approved`, `Approved with required revision`, or `Not approved`.
- Record required wording changes precisely. Do not enter operational values in `04-consent-and-privacy.md` until the owner supplies approved values.
- Field use remains blocked until every applicable item is approved, all required revisions are incorporated, and the operational fields are completed.
